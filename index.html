BridgerLand

Launched in 2021 by Zackaria and BedlessNoob, BridgerLand began as a place to master bridging with FastBridger. It has since grown into a polished network with additional modes like the highly‑popular FFA. I lead development and community operations to keep everything running smoothly and evolving.

Visit BridgerLand

Sword Network

Originally the cracked edition of BridgerLand, Sword served the same purpose for a wider audience. After closing due to costs, we returned in 2023 with a fresh brand and focus. Today Sword offers FastBridger, Practice PvP, and more, with a sustainable approach to growth.

Visit Sword
